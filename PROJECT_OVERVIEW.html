🚀 How It Works

1

Open the PWA on Your Phone

Install from your browser to the home screen. Works on iOS, Android, and desktop. The app is always available, even offline.

2

Pick a Subject & Start Learning

Choose from your 6 Year 12 subjects. Each has 4 units aligned with the QCAA syllabus, broken into digestible topics.

3

Take Quick Quizzes

5-minute quiz sessions with multiple choice, fill-in-blank, and matching questions. Get instant feedback and explanations.

4

Chat with the AI Coach

Stuck on a concept? The MCP-powered AI tutor uses Socratic questioning to guide you to understanding without just giving answers.

5

Track Your Progress

View mastery levels per topic, maintain your study streak, and see what the spaced repetition algorithm recommends you review next.

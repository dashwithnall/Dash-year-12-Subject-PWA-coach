🚀 How It Works

1

Open the PWA on Your Phone

Install from browser to home screen. Works on iOS, Android, and desktop - even offline.

2

Pick a Subject & Start Learning

Choose from 6 subjects. Each has 4 units aligned with QCAA 2025 syllabus.

3

Take Quick Quizzes

5-minute sessions with MCQ, fill-in-blank, and matching. Instant feedback.

4

Chat with AI Coach

MCP-powered AI uses Socratic questioning to guide you to understanding.

5

Track Your Progress

View mastery levels, maintain streaks, see spaced repetition recommendations.
